Dr. Hardik SoniFOUNDER, CALYSTA PRO EMR

Running an aesthetic practice asks you to be many things. A physician. A leader. A business owner. And, always, someone who cares.

Through his experience in minimally invasive and non-invasive cosmetic procedures, Dr. Hardik Soni came to understand the distinct challenges of running and growing an aesthetic practice.

He partnered with a software development team to create Calysta Pro EMR: a solution shaped around that firsthand understanding, with aesthetic practices at the center.

The vision remains simple. Help practices navigate the business of care, so they can put more attention into the people they care for.
