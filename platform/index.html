Getting to know Calysta Pro.

Who is Calysta Pro designed for?

Calysta Pro is an electronic medical record solution focused on aesthetic practices. It was founded by Dr. Hardik Soni, drawing on his experience in aesthetic medicine.

What makes its approach different?

Its focus is aesthetic medicine. The company’s stated vision combines simplicity, ongoing development, and collaboration with the practices it serves.

Are these screens the actual product?

No. This website is an independent redesign concept. The screens are illustrative explorations using fictional data. Current capabilities should be confirmed directly with Calysta Pro.

Can I book a real demo through this preview?

The demo page lets you explore a proposed inquiry experience. It does not send or save requests. Existing clients can access the original Calysta Pro website using the Client login link.
